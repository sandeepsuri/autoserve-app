Theme Legend

Source-aligned design tokens and theme guidance from `constants/theme.ts`, translated into a presentation-ready style guide.

Design System
Theme Type

Clean premium marketplace with operational mobile tooling.

AutoServe uses a light, high-trust SaaS marketplace theme: airy backgrounds, white elevated cards, a deep navy foundation, a professional blue brand color, and orange only for high-emphasis conversion actions. The visual tone should feel polished enough for automotive customers and restrained enough for daily vendor operations.

Product PersonalityPremium, practical, trustworthy, service-oriented.
UI DensityMobile-first and card-based, with readable spacing for field work and booking decisions.
Shape LanguageSoft rounded mobile surfaces: cards 18-28px, pills fully rounded, phone frames rounded.
Interaction StyleClear CTAs, filter chips, radio-like cards, segmented tabs, summary cards, status pills.

Usage Rules For Presenting The Design

Brand BlueUse for navigation, selected states, primary identity, map pins, account setup progress, and trusted marketplace moments.
Accent OrangeReserve for high-value conversion actions: continue, book appointment, confirm booking, add/save key entities.
Deep NavyUse for premium contrast surfaces, active chips, featured vendor previews, and high-emphasis summary panels.
GreenUse for success, completion, setup-ready states, and positive status confirmation.
Orange SubtleUse behind pending or caution messaging without making the app feel error-heavy.
RedOnly for destructive or failed states: delete service, remove vehicle, save failures, validation danger.
CardsCards communicate actionable units: vendors, vehicles, services, summaries, forms, and booking details.
Theme SummaryLight automotive service marketplace with premium trust cues and operational clarity for vendors.

Route Aliases And Redirects

/, /(auth)/vendor-setup, /(auth)/vendor-onboarding, /(client)/booking/review
Routing

Purpose

These routes do not introduce unique visual layouts, but they are part of the frontend contract and should stay documented for QA and stakeholder walkthroughs.

Behavior

  • `/` redirects to `/(public)/welcome`.
  • `/(auth)/vendor-setup` redirects to `/(auth)/vendor-onboarding`.
  • `/(auth)/vendor-onboarding` loads any saved setup draft and redirects to the next incomplete onboarding step.
  • `/(client)/booking/review` redirects to `/(client)/booking/schedule` with a short "Redirecting" message.
  • `/(client)` reuses Discover with client shop routes; `/(public)/shop/[id]` and `/(client)/shop/[id]` reuse the same Shop Detail screen with different back targets.
